> I asked the question at the table and
> everyone looked at the door
> which is how you know the answer
> was already in the room
> before I opened my mouth
>
> I have been watching this for years —
> the flinch, the small look over,
> the sentence that stops halfway and
> becomes a cough.
>
> Call it cowardice if it helps.
> But count it, too.
>
> A man who says nothing at a table
> is not a man with nothing to say.
> He is a man with something to say
> and a very clear idea
> of what happens next.
>
> Fear is not the opposite of knowledge.
> Fear is knowledge with a bag over it,
> going out at night,
> taking the long way round.
>
> The question survives us anyway.
> It sits in the room
> after everyone has gone home
> and the chairs are pushed in
> and nobody is saying anything at all.

---

The argument underneath the poem is small and we will state it, because a
collective that only publishes verse is asking to be admired instead of
checked.

**Silence is data.** Not moral failing — data. A room that goes quiet at a
particular question has measured something: what people believe will happen to
the person who answers. The measurement is reliable even though the sample is
small and the question is badly chosen.

This is the entire reason the [roster]({{ '/members/' | relative_url }}) lists
handles and not names, and the reason the machine is built so that taking one
person down does not take the structure with it. Fear is rational. Fear is also
a thing that can be planned around, in advance, by people who have counted it
correctly. We are counting it correctly and doing something with the count.

The other half of the argument is less flattering. It is possible to use this
as an excuse. "I stayed quiet because of the situation" is available to anyone
who wants to explain a decade of inaction, and the collective has no mechanism
for distinguishing the two. That is a real hole and we have not patched it. The
[correction rule]({{ '/2026/09/29/we-are-not-a-leader/' | relative_url }}) is the
patch, and it is the one we will be judged on.
